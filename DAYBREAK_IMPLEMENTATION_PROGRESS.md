## Design Patterns Applied

### Colors
- **Backgrounds**: `bg-dawn-100`, gradient-to-br from-gold-200 via-dawn-100 to-sunset-200
- **Text**: `text-bark-500`, `text-bark-300`, `text-bark-200`
- **Accents**: `text-sunset-500`, `bg-gold-400`, `border-sunset-400`

### Components
- **Cards**: `card`, `card-elevated`
- **Buttons**: `btn-primary`, `btn-secondary`, `btn-ghost`
- **Inputs**: `input`, `select`, `textarea`
- **Typography**: `text-h1`, `text-h2`, `text-body`, `text-caption`

### Micro-interactions
- **Animations**: `animate-bounce-in`, `animate-icon-bounce`, `hover-lift`
- **Shadows**: `shadow-warm-sm/md/lg`, `shadow-glow-sunset`
- **Transitions**: `transition-all duration-fast`
